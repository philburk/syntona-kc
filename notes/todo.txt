unsubscribe, subscribeJob, and subscriptionGeneration are plain fields written from both the
caller's thread and the module's coroutines. The generation check closes the logical race,
but strictly these are unsynchronized reads on JVM. Same shape as the existing MidiOutModule
fields, so I kept it consistent rather than diverging — worth confining the module's coroutines
to a single-threaded dispatcher if you want that airtight.
